01 / ABOUT

About me

I am a PhD candidate at the School of Astronomy, Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM), Iran.

My research focuses on classical Cepheid variable stars and their application to the extragalactic distance scale. I am particularly interested in the use of infrared observations to study Cepheid pulsation, improve period–luminosity relations, and investigate systematic and random uncertainties in distance measurements.

I work with multi-wavelength observations and time-series data, including observations from NEOWISE and SPHEREx, alongside optical and near-infrared datasets of Cepheids in the Magellanic Clouds.

02 / RESEARCH

Research interests

My work uses variable stars to investigate stellar physics and the calibration of the cosmic distance scale.

01

Cepheids and the distance scale

Studying the period–luminosity relation and its use in measuring extragalactic distances and constraining the Hubble constant.

Distance calibration
02

Infrared Cepheid observations

Using mid-infrared light curves to examine pulsation behaviour, mean magnitudes, and the influence of atmospheric effects.

NEOWISE · SPHEREx
03

Baade–Wesselink methods

Investigating the relationship between infrared light-curve variations and spectroscopic radial-velocity measurements.

Pulsation physics
04

Magellanic Cloud Cepheids

Combining multi-wavelength photometry to study Cepheid properties, extinction, and the three-dimensional structure of the Magellanic Clouds.

LMC · SMC

04 / PROJECTS

Current research projects

A

NEOWISE observations of the Large Magellanic Cloud

Mid-infrared Cepheid light curves, period–luminosity relations, and the effects of CO absorption in the W2 band.

NEOWISE · W1/W2 · Cepheids · LMC

B

Infrared light curves and radial-velocity variation

Exploring how the first derivative of infrared light curves compares with spectroscopic radial-velocity curves, and assessing the physical interpretation and limitations of this approach.

Baade–Wesselink · Radial velocity · Infrared

C

Multi-wavelength structure of the Magellanic Clouds

Combining optical, near-infrared, and mid-infrared Cepheid observations to investigate extinction, individual distances, and the three-dimensional structure of the Magellanic Clouds.

OGLE · VMC · Spitzer · NEOWISE

D

SPHEREx spectroscopy of Cepheids

Using low-resolution near- and mid-infrared spectra to investigate molecular absorption features and their relation to Cepheid pulsation.

SPHEREx · Molecular bands · Stellar atmospheres
